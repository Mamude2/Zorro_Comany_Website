Who We Are

A diversified industrial group rooted in Nigerian enterprise

ZORRO Industries Group Limited is a Nigerian-owned diversified industrial company established in 2023 and headquartered in Tudun Yola, Kano State. We provide innovative industrial solutions spanning manufacturing, construction support, industrial supplies, machinery importation, furniture and interior decor, agriculture and commercial services.

From our base in Kano, we serve clients across Nigeria and the wider African market — combining local insight with international sourcing standards to deliver dependable, quality-driven outcomes on every project.
